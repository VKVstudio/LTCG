Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

The cost of obtaining a residence permit in Armenia through Legal and Tax Consulting Group LLC starts from $800. This includes preparation of all documents, translation, notarization, and submission to the migration service. The state fee is paid separately (~105,000 AMD). We will calculate the exact cost during a free consultation.
To obtain a residence permit you will need: a valid passport, application form, photographs, proof of grounds (employment contract, business registration, enrollment in education, etc.), medical certificate, criminal background check, and proof of accommodation. Legal and Tax Consulting Group LLC handles the preparation and translation of all documents.
LLC registration in Armenia takes 2–3 business days. You will need: articles of association, a resolution on incorporation, founders' details, and a legal address. LTCG handles the entire process — from document preparation to obtaining the registration certificate and tax registration. The cost starts from $500.
IT companies with an IT certificate can benefit from preferential rates: employee income tax — 10% (instead of 20%), corporate income tax — 0%. Standard rates: VAT — 20%, income tax — 20%, corporate income tax — 18%. Legal and Tax Consulting Group LLC will help you obtain an IT certificate and optimize your tax burden.
Generally, banks require personal presence. However, some banks allow remote account opening for legal entities with a notarized power of attorney. Legal and Tax Consulting Group LLC will help you choose the right bank and prepare all necessary documents.
Accounting services from Legal and Tax Consulting Group LLC include: bookkeeping and primary documentation, tax return preparation and filing, payroll calculations and contributions, financial reporting, and tax advisory consultations. The cost starts from $100/month.
